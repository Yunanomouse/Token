What this is. A paper-trading simulator. It replays real daily closing prices from 2006 to 2018 and trades them the way the Quantum Trading engine would: every rebalance it re-estimates returns and risk from the look-back window, picks the portfolio, checks the risk limits, and fills orders at that day's close less the fee. No real money, no exchange, no slippage, so real results would be worse.

        
How to read it. The orange line is the plain alternative of holding every selected stock in equal amounts. If the blue line is not clearly above it, the optimiser did not earn its keep over that period.

        
Prices: adjusted daily closes from PyPortfolioOpt's test data (MIT licence), originally Yahoo Finance.
