This is an estimate, not tax advice. It applies progressive federal and provincial brackets, the basic personal amount (including the federal enhanced-BPA phase-out), the Ontario surtax and health premium, the Quebec abatement, and employee CPP/QPP, CPP2, EI and QPIP. It ignores most other credits, deductions and provincial low-income reductions, and it assumes the full amount is employment income.

Figures come from data/json/ in this repository, collected 2026-08-18. Verify against CRA or Revenu Quebec before relying on any number for filing or payroll.
