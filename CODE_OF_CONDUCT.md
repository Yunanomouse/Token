# Community guidelines and Code of Conduct

Last updated: 2026-10-13

Token is a project about Canadian taxes and trading. People come here with real money questions, so the community needs to be respectful, accurate and careful with personal information.

## Our standards

Do:

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people new to taxes, trading or Python.
- Back up tax claims with a source, such as a CRA page, the Income Tax Act or a published table. Say clearly when something is your opinion or an estimate.
- Credit other people's work.
- Accept corrections gracefully. A wrong tax figure is a bug to fix, not a personal failing.

Don't:

- Harass, insult or discriminate against anyone, or use sexual language or imagery.
- Post anyone's personal or financial information. That includes your own broker exports, account numbers, Social Insurance Numbers and tax returns, and anyone else's.
- Present yourself as giving professional tax, legal or investment advice in project spaces, or promise someone a tax result.
- Promote trading signals, pump schemes, paid groups or referral links.
- Spam, troll, or post off-topic content.

## Scope

These guidelines apply in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and any chat or forum run for Token. They also apply when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

To report a problem, open an issue at https://github.com/Yunanomouse/Token/issues. If the report is sensitive, use GitHub's "Report content" option on the comment, or ask in an issue for a private way to reach the maintainer. Don't include personal details in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed promptly, and the reporter's identity kept private where possible.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block people who break these guidelines. Depending on how serious and repeated the problem is, steps range from a warning, to a temporary ban, to a permanent ban.

## Contributing

- Open an issue before a large change.
- Keep pull requests focused.
- For tax data changes, include the source link and the date you checked it.
- Don't add analytics, tracking or session replay code. The `No session replay` CI check blocks it ([Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md)).
