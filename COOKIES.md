# Cookie Policy and managing cookies

Last updated: 2026-10-13

## Does Token use cookies?

No. Token is code and data you run on your own computer. It has no website of its own, so it sets no cookies, pixels, local storage or other trackers, and there is nothing to accept or reject.

## Cookies from sites you visit

Websites you use alongside Token set their own cookies under their own policies:

- **GitHub**, where this repository is hosted. See [GitHub's cookie documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-cookies). GitHub provides its own cookie settings.
- **Yahoo Finance and other data providers**, if you visit their sites. The optional OpenBB script requests data from them but does not store browser cookies.
- **Any page linked from the docs**, such as CRA or TaxTips.ca.

## How to manage cookies in your browser

You can view, block or delete cookies for any site in your browser settings:

- **Chrome:** Settings, then Privacy and security, then Third-party cookies or Delete browsing data.
- **Firefox:** Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Cookies and Site Data.
- **Safari (Mac):** Settings, then Privacy, then Manage Website Data. On iPhone: Settings, then Safari.
- **Edge:** Settings, then Cookies and site permissions.

Blocking all cookies can stop some sites, including GitHub sign-in, from working.

## If Token ever has a website

If a landing page, waitlist or hosted planner launches, it will start with no non-essential cookies, and session replay stays off for every visitor ([Privacy Policy, section 2](PRIVACY.md#2-session-replay-is-off-for-everyone)). If analytics or marketing cookies are added later, the site will ask for your consent first and include a "Manage cookies" link, and this page will list each cookie, its purpose and how long it lasts.
